How anyone can check their medicine in 3 steps

No complicated apps or technical knowledge required. It works on any phone.

1

Find the square code

Look at the back of your medicine foil strip or side of the box for the small square QR code.

2

Take a photo

Open our free scanner and snap a clear photo of the code with your phone camera, or type the code.

3

See instant results

You will immediately see a big green checkmark if it is genuine, or a clear red warning if it is fake.

Why counterfeiters cannot cheat this system

Anyone can copy a barcode label, but fake sellers are completely locked out of Vite Pill. Medicine companies cannot simply sign up on the internet. Health inspectors personally check the company's government drug manufacturing license and create their login for them. Every genuine pill pack is linked directly to that approved company.
