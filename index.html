How restricted provisioning stops counterfeiting

Traditional barcodes only track product categories. Counterfeiters exploit this by printing identical labels on fake drugs. Vite Pill closes this loophole through three safeguards:

Admin-issued accounts only

Counterfeiters cannot self-register. The regulatory administrator inspects Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) licenses before manually creating manufacturer logins.

Individual package serialization

Instead of a shared barcode, every blister pack receives a unique 16-hex code and micro-QR permanently attributed to the issuing pharmaceutical corporation.

Instant patient verification

Patients and retail pharmacists check authenticity in under a second on any device with zero scripts, receiving immediate drug facts or counterfeit warnings.

Android inspection application

Lightweight inspection tool for pharmacists, clinical dispensaries, and patient verification in low-connectivity settings.
